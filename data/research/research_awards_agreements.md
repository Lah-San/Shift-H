## 2. ANF Agreement 2024 – Registered Nurses, Midwives, Enrolled (Mental Health) Nurses

### 2.1 Hours of work (cl 28)
- **Ordinary hours**: average 38/week; **actual hours worked 40/week or 80/fortnight**, with 2 hours/week accruing to a maximum of **12 ADOs per 12 months** (hospital), or 0.4 hr/day accruing to the 20th day off in a 4-week cycle (hospital or community), or other agreed arrangement (cl 28(2)).
- **Daily maximum**: ordinary hours in any one day are a **maximum of eight**; **no broken shifts**; **10-hour night shifts** may be worked without overtime by agreement (cl 28(10)). **12-hour rosters** are only via a local flexibility agreement under cl 29 using the parties' template 12-hour roster agreement (cl 29(4)) with a **minimum four-week lead time** (cl 29(3)(e)).
- **Breaks between shifts**: **20 hours free** when changing to or from night and day duty (cl 28(11)); **9.5 hours** when changing from evening to day duty (cl 28(12)). After overtime a **9.5-hour break** is required or double time applies until released (cl 30(4)); same 9.5-hour rule after recall while on call (cl 31(7)).
- **Shift combinations**: a full-time employee will not be required to work a combination of shifts exceeding all night, day or evening shifts, or both day and evening shifts, in either week of the roster (cl 28(13)).
- **Days off**: not less than **two full days per week or four per fortnight**; where practicable consecutive and **not preceded by a night shift** unless the employee is rostered on evening/night immediately after the days off (cl 28(14)).
- **Consecutive duties**: not more than **seven consecutive duties** where practicable unless the employee requests and employer approves; **no more than ten duties per fortnight**; for 10-hour nights a **maximum of five consecutive** (unless requested) and **no more than eight 10-hour nights per fortnight** (cl 28(15)).
- **Senior RN/M Levels 5-10**: no fixed hours; hours over 152 per 4-week cycle become TOIL at overtime rates, to be taken within eight weeks (cl 28(6)-(8)).
- **Part-time**: minimum weekly hours fixed at commencement and only varied by written agreement; 41+ hrs/fortnight paid pro-rata to 40 hrs with ADO accrual; 40 or less pro-rata to 38 (cl 14(3)). Overtime for part-timers only for hours beyond the unit's full-time shift length or >38 hrs/week; otherwise extra hours are paid at ordinary time with pro-rata accrual credit or with the casual loading (cl 30(2), 30(3)(d)).
- **Casual**: hourly, engagement period of 12 weeks or less, **25% loading**, **minimum two hours**, one hour's notice; penalties calculated on the ordinary rate (cl 14(1)). Default full-time shift length for casual overtime is ten hours (cl 30(8)(c)).
- **Meal breaks**: minimum 30 minutes unpaid; not more than five hours without a meal break (six if starting at or before 0700; 5.5 hrs on a ten-hour shift); two paid 10-minute refreshment breaks per shift (cl 32).

### 2.2 Rostering (cl 28(18)-(27))
- A **14-day roster will ordinarily be posted 28 days** before the first working period; in extenuating circumstances **not less than 14 days** prior (cl 28(18)).
- A roster **may be altered at any time** to carry on the nursing/midwifery service **where another employee is absent on account of illness, or if hospital emergencies render it necessary**; if the alteration has an employee working on a rostered day off (other than an ADO) it is paid as **overtime** under cl 30 (cl 28(18)). Employer will notify the employee before the changed shift where practicable (cl 28(19)).
- **ADO roster** posted at least four weeks before it operates; ADOs offered within 28 days of completing each accrual cycle; employer may roster up to 12 single ADOs per year; ADOs may be substituted by agreement (cl 28(3)-(4)).
- **Permanent night shift employees** (rostered permanent nights over seven days): must be consulted (two-week consultation period) before being rostered to day/afternoon; failing agreement, **minimum four weeks' written notice**; can be directed to a **maximum two weeks per year** of day/afternoon shifts (cl 28(23)-(26)).
- Reasonable consideration to flexibility requests due to caring responsibilities; temporary reduction of hours requests (cl 28(20)-(21)).
- On-call cannot be rostered while on leave, the day before leave, on ADOs or the day before ADOs, except by mutual agreement (cl 31(16)).

### 2.3 Annual leave (cl 33)
- **20 days (4 weeks) per 12 months** continuous service (cl 33(1)(a)); accrues **pro-rata weekly** and is cumulative (cl 33(2)); cannot accumulate for more than **two years** except with employee consent (cl 33(3)).
- **Employer must respond within 14 days** of the application; must not unreasonably refuse; refusal must be in writing with reasons within 14 days; if "operational requirements" is the reason, details must be given (cl 33(1)(b)-(e)).
- **Additional leave**: **continuous shift employees** (rostered afternoon and/or night with day shift over any days, or permanent nights over 7 days) accrue up to **five additional days** by table (3 months = 1 day ... 12 months = 5 days) (cl 33(8)-(9)); employees **regularly rostered on call** on weekends/public holidays/days off (min 2 days per 4-week cycle) accrue up to **five days** (4 cycles = 1 day ... 12 cycles = 5 days) (cl 33(10)-(11)); **combined maximum five days** (cl 33(14)). **North of the 26th parallel: +1 week** (cl 33(15)).
- **Taking leave**: after 12 months, in one continuous period or **two periods of not less than two weeks** (cl 33(16)); by agreement in **multiple portions of not less than one day** (cl 33(18)); leave in advance with approval (cl 33(17)); **double the period at half pay** by agreement (cl 33(19)).
- **Excess leave**: accrued leave **in excess of 10 weeks** will be taken at the operational convenience of the employer, **minimum five days**, except where the employee had a leave application denied in the preceding 12 months (cl 33(20)-(21)).
- **Payment**: ordinary salary plus the higher of a **17.5% loading** or the average shift/weekend penalties of the six completed pay periods before leave (capped) (cl 33(22)). No ADO accrual during the first four weeks of annual leave (cl 33(5)).
- **Public holiday during annual leave**: a day's leave in lieu at ordinary pay (cl 37(6)). Employees get 11 public holidays; 50% loading for ordinary hours worked on a public holiday (casual 150%) (cl 37(1),(3),(4)).
- **Purchased leave (42/52)**: 42 weeks' salary = 10 weeks purchased leave, through to 51/52 = 1 week (cl 46(1)); refusal reasons limited to **availability of suitable leave cover, impact on service delivery, or existing leave liability**, written reasons within 14 days; employer will ensure adequate staffing so purchased leave can be taken; taken in 12-month blocks, no loading (cl 46(2)-(13)).
- **Deferred salary scheme**: 80% salary over four years, fifth year off (cl 47).
- Cashing out of leave (cl 41) also exists.

### 2.4 Personal leave (cl 38)
- **15 days per year** credited as 8.5 days on appointment, 6.5 days at six months, then 15 days each anniversary; unused leave **cumulative** (cl 38(7)-(8)); may be taken **hourly** (cl 38(10)); paid at base rate excluding penalties (cl 38(2)).
- Covers illness/injury, **carer's leave**, unanticipated matters, and pre-approved planned matters (cl 38(20)).
- **Evidence**: required for absences **exceeding two consecutive working days**; generally not for one or two days, though the employer may request it with reasons (cl 38(25)-(26)). Reasonable notice; where not possible, notice as early as possible on the day (cl 38(24)).
- Can anticipate up to five days after 12 months' service (cl 38(18)(a)); annual leave re-credited if confined for at least seven days with medical evidence (cl 38(30)); fixed-term of 12 months or more get full credit, shorter contracts pro rata (cl 38(5)).

### 2.5 Long service leave (cl 35)
- **13 weeks after 10 years**, then **13 weeks after each further seven years**; **pro-rata access after seven years** in the first period (cl 35(1)).
- May be cleared in **minimum periods of one day**, at half pay (double period) or double pay (half period) (cl 35(2)).
- Taken at a time convenient to the employer with **not less than 30 days' notice** to the employee; if no agreement the employee may give **eight weeks' written notice** to take the full entitlement (only after six months from accrual) (cl 35(9)). Refusal in writing within 14 days with reasons (cl 35(4)-(5)). No ADO accrual while on LSL (cl 35(6)).

### 2.6 Professional development and study (cl 55, 56, 60)
- **PDL: two days per year** (from 1 July, non-cumulative) for full-time; **+1 day** for Nurse Practitioners, dual-registered nurse/midwives, endorsed midwives and RN prescribers (max one extra); **graduates +3 days** on commencement; remote: 150-400 km from Perth GPO +1 day, >400 km +2 days per year; part-time pro-rata; recorded and acquitted in hours; applications in advance; not to be used for employer-mandated competencies (cl 56).
- **Study leave**: up to **five hours per week** paid time off for approved part-time study (cl 55).
- **Professional development allowance**: $700 (L1.1-1.7), $1,100 (L1.8-2.4 and SRN 1-5), $1,400 (SRN 6-10) per year, paid fortnightly (cl 60).

### 2.7 Overtime, penalties, on call (cl 26, 30, 31)
- **Shift penalties** on ordinary rate: **afternoon 15%** (commencing not earlier than 1200 and finishing after 1800), **night 35%** (1830-0730 weekdays), **Saturday 50%**, **Sunday 75%** (continues to 0730 Monday); weekend rates substitute for, not add to, afternoon/night rates (cl 26(1)-(5)). A Monday-Friday day worker required to work Sunday gets **double time** (cl 26(7)).
- **Overtime**: Monday-Saturday **time and a half for the first three hours, double time thereafter**; **Sunday double time**; **public holiday double time and a half**; TOIL by election; overtime can be commuted to an annual amount by agreement (cl 30(1),(3),(7)).
- **Recall**: minimum **three hours at overtime rates** (cl 31(1)); **on-call allowance 18.75%** of the Level 1.3 RN hourly base rate per hour, **+50% on public holidays** (cl 31(10)-(11)); telephone advice while on call = one hour overtime (cl 31(13)).

### 2.8 Employment modes, fixed term, acting (cl 13, 14, 16, 23)
- **Higher duties**: paid for any period of **one day or more** in a higher classified position, equal to the salary difference (proportionate if partial duties); service increments after 12 months' acting in 18; annual leave accrued during 12+ months' acting is paid at the higher rate (cl 23(1)). **Shift coordination allowance**: a Level 1 directed to act as Shift Coordinator for a whole shift is paid at Level 2.1 (cl 23(2)).
- **Permanency and relief cover** (cl 16): permanent direct employment is preferred; fixed term, casual and agency are not preferred; **"all leave will ordinarily be covered"** unless cover is shown not operationally necessary (cl 16(1)(f)); each hospital will **employ permanent relief staff or sufficient FTE for planned annual leave and ADO relief** and **maintain a casual pool for short-term work** (cl 16(1)(g)); **agency only if no other suitably qualified employees are available in the short term** and reviewed every three months (cl 16(2)); fixed term contracts only for listed situations (cl 16(3)).
- Notice of termination 2-4 weeks by service; Level 2+ four weeks; probation three months (cl 13).

### 2.9 Workload, ratios and NHpPD (Part 2, cl 10-12)
- **Cl 10 Nurse/Midwife to Patient Ratios**: the employer commits to minimum ratios via a phased approach over at least two years (the **WA Ratio Model**), which replaces NHpPD (cl 10(1)-(3)); a Taskforce guides implementation; where NHpPD gives more nurses per shift, that level is retained (cl 10(7)(b)); the **coordinator is in principle excluded from the ratio** (cl 10(7)(d)); qualified babies count as patients (cl 10(7)(e)); a four-stage dispute procedure (line manager within the shift -> nursing executive -> HSP/Hospital Steering Committee -> WAIRC) applies four months after a ward goes live (cl 10(9)-(10)); ratios count RNs, midwives and ENs only (cl 10(11)).
- **Cl 11 Workload management**: employer must ensure staffing levels appropriate for safe care, must take reasonable steps so employees do not work excessive hours and **can take the leave provided for in the Agreement** (cl 11(3)(b)); workload grievance grounds include being denied access to the PD quota within 12 months (cl 11(4)(c)); a written response within 24 hours if requested (cl 11(5)(c)).
- **Cl 12 NHpPD** applies in areas where the Ratio Model is not yet applied, reproducing the 2001 Exceptional Matters Order (Schedule A) with category determinations published and notified to the ANF within 21 days.
- **Actual ratio figures** are set by the mandatory WA Health *Nurse Midwife to Patient Ratios Policy* (MP 0187/24, effective 30 September 2024, applying to CAHS, EMHS, NMHS, SMHS metropolitan medical/surgical wards and critical care areas), not by the Agreement. Per WA Health news/ANMJ reporting **[NOT VERIFIED against the policy PDF, which returned 404]**: medical/surgical wards **1:4 plus a hands-free shift coordinator on morning and afternoon shifts, 1:7 at night**; emergency departments **1:3** (PCH ED pilot July 2023); phases: SCGH/Osborne Park Oct 2024; metro med/surg wards at PCH, FSH, Fremantle, Rockingham, Armadale, SCGH, OPH, RPH from July 2025; critical care (ICU, CCU, neonatal, paediatrics) from February 2026; the next stage (remaining EDs and specialist wards) is reported as postponed with no revised timeline (ANMJ, 14 Sep 2026).

## 3. UWU Enrolled Nurses / AIN / Aboriginal Health Practitioners / Health Workers Agreement 2024

### 3.1 Hours (cl 18), breaks (cl 19), rosters (cl 20)
- Ordinary hours **average 38/week over any five days of the week or 10 days of the fortnight**, in one of: 19-day month (8-hr days, 0.4 hr/day to an ADO); **76 hours over nine days per fortnight** with the tenth day unpaid RDO; **40/week or 80/fortnight with up to 12 ADOs per year**; or straight 38/76 (cl 18.1). The first two cycles only by agreement (cl 18.2).
- Where practicable ordinary hours rostered over **not more than six consecutive days** (cl 18.4). Employees working **less than 16 hrs/week do not accrue ADOs** (cl 15.4).
- **ADOs**: minimum five consecutive days, or single days by mutual agreement (cl 18.9); where 11+ ADOs are accumulated the employer may direct ADOs with **24 hours' notice (one day) or two weeks' notice (two or more consecutive)** to reduce the bank to 10 (cl 18.12); ADO roster posted **four weeks** ahead (cl 18.14); **permanent relief pools** may be introduced to backfill planned leave (cl 18.20).
- **Meal break** min 30 min; not more than **5.5 hours** without a meal break; overtime rates if delayed >30 min (cl 19).
- **Days off**: minimum **two full consecutive days per week or four per fortnight**; missed days off not taken within four weeks are **added to annual leave** (cl 20.1).
- **Roster posting**: **14 days** where practicable, **never less than seven days** (cl 20.3); rosters may be altered at any time if hospital exigencies require (cl 20.2).
- **Shift change**: **20 hours** free when changing night<->day; **9.5 hours** evening->day (**8 hours** in country hospitals below regional level) (cl 20.4); shift-combination rule as ANF (cl 20.5); these do not apply where the employee must cover an absence or emergency (cl 20.8).
- **Daily max**: **eight hours** ordinary for EN/AIN/AHP, **no broken shifts**, **10-hour nights by agreement** (cl 20.6); Health Worker spread of hours max 10 (cl 20.7).
- **Shifts**: **no more than five per week or 10 per fortnight**; night duty may be varied to 4/8 by agreement with the Union; work on a day off in an emergency is overtime (cl 20.9).
- **Part-time**: pro-rata to 38 (or 40 where ADO cycle); employer may propose increased hours in a roster period with **one clear day's notice and employee agreement** (cl 15.5); Health Workers minimum **three consecutive hours** per shift (cl 15.6(c)); hours beyond agreed = overtime (cl 15.6(d)).
- **Casual**: **25% loading**, **minimum two hours** (cl 14.1-14.2); casual Health Workers only for unplanned short-term work (cl 14.5).
- **Flexibility in hours** (12-hr shifts etc.) via representative forum and **secret ballot**, Union notified 14 days before the ballot, **four weeks' lead time** (cl 21).

### 3.2 Annual leave (cl 41)
- **EN, AIN, AHP: seven consecutive weeks** per 12 months (cl 41.1(a)); Enrolled Community Nurses, Enrolled Community School Nurses and **Health Workers: four weeks** (cl 41.1(b)); Health Worker shift workers +1 week if regularly rotating, or +1 day per seven weeks of non-rotating afternoon/night (max 5) (cl 41.12). Accrues pro-rata weekly, cumulative (cl 41.1(c)).
- **Employer responds within 14 days** (cl 41.1(e)).
- **Payment** for EN/AIN/AHP is the greater of: wage including the penalties they would have received (or 12-week average), or **18.75% loading on 5/7ths** of the leave with 2/7ths at penalty-inclusive rate (cl 41.2). Health Workers: **17.5% loading** on up to four weeks (five for shift workers) (cl 41.3).
- **Taking**: multiple portions by agreement, **including up to five single days, but at least one portion of not less than two consecutive weeks** (cl 41.5); double at half pay (cl 41.6); leave granted as soon as practicable after falling due and **not accumulated except with employee consent** (cl 41.8); **employee to be given at least two weeks' notice of the date leave is to be taken** unless a lesser period is agreed (cl 41.9).
- **Excess leave**: employer may give notice where credits exceed **two years' entitlement**; within 30 days agree a program to reduce excess **within 24 months** (cl 41.13).
- **Purchased leave 42/52** (cl 53) and **deferred wages 80%/5 years** (cl 54).
- **Public holidays**: 11 days; **50% loading** for EN/AIN/AHP working a public holiday (Easter Sunday 75%) (cl 43.4).

### 3.3 Personal leave (cl 47)
- **114 hours per year** (64.6 hrs on appointment, 49.4 hrs at six months, then 114 hrs per anniversary), cumulative, hourly access (cl 47.2-47.6). Health Workers' sick/carer's leave includes shift and weekend penalties (cl 47.7).
- Notice within **24 hours** of commencement of absence (cl 47.15); **evidence for absences exceeding two consecutive working days** (cl 47.16). Anticipate up to 38 hours (cl 47.10(b)).

### 3.4 Long service leave (cl 44)
- **13 weeks after 10 years, then after each further seven years**; minimum periods of one day; half or double pay options; no ADO accrual on LSL (cl 44.1-44.3).

### 3.5 Professional development leave (cl 64)
- EN/AIN/AHP: **16 hours per year** non-cumulative from 1 July; **graduates +24 hours** on commencement; remote **+8 hours** (150-400 km) or **+16 hours** (>400 km) on commencement and each year; part-time pro-rata; not for mandatory competencies; equal access across nursing levels (cl 64.2-64.15).

### 3.6 Overtime, penalties, on call, higher duties (cl 22, 23, 25, 27)
- **Overtime**: Monday-Saturday **time and a half for the first two hours, double time thereafter**; Sunday double time; **work on a rostered day off = double time** (cl 22.1); TOIL granted within 28 days or paid (cl 22.2); **10-hour break** after overtime or double time until released (8 hours for shift changeover) (cl 22.4); Health Worker recall min three hours (cl 22.1(f)).
- **On call**: 18.75% of 1/38th of the ASEN Level 3 weekly rate = **$8.07/hr from 7 Oct 2024, $8.39 (2025), $8.71 (2026)**; +50% on public holidays (cl 23.1).
- **Shift penalties**: afternoon **15%** (starts on/after 1200, finishes after 1800), night **35%** (EN/AIN/AHP: shift commencing 2030-0400; Health Workers: completed 1800-0730), Saturday **50%**, Sunday **75%** (to 0730 Monday) (cl 27.2-27.8).
- **Higher duties**: an EN on duty while the Health Service Manager/RN is on call is paid the **ASEN 1 rate for the entire shift** (cl 25.1); Health Workers/AHPs on higher duties >2 hours are paid the higher rate for the whole shift (cl 25.2).

## 4. HSUWA – PACTS Industrial Agreement 2024 (allied health, pharmacy, science, technical, clerical)

### 4.1 Hours (cl 16), safe rostering (cl 17), rostering (cl 18)
- Ordinary hours **average 38/week, generally five days of 7 h 36 min**; alternatives: **19-day month** (152 hrs/4 weeks over 19 x 8-hr days) or **nine-day fortnight** (76 hrs over nine days of 8 h 27 min) (cl 16.1(b)); non-shift span **0600-1800 Monday-Friday** (cl 16.1(c)); **no split shifts** (cl 16.1(e)).
- **Meal breaks** 30-60 min; not more than five hours without a break (six on 11/12-hr shifts) (cl 16.2).
- **Shifts of up to 12 hours** only by written agreement, average 38/week over the cycle, **minimum one clear day off in each eight days**; HSUWA advised beforehand (cl 16.4). Flexitime: **max 10 ordinary hours per day**; 4-week settlement period 144-168 hours; credit cap 20 hrs, carry-over 16; debit carry-over 8 (cl 16.6).
- **Changing rostered/flexi days off** for pressing operational reasons: with **less than three days' notice the employer pays overtime** unless the employee freely proposes an alternative (cl 16.8(b)).
- **Flexible work requests**: response in writing within **14 days**; employer withdrawal on **14 days' notice**; operational requirements limited to leave cover, cost, service impact, impact on other employees, leave liability (cl 16.3(g)-(h)).
- **Safe rostering** (cl 17): reasonable consideration to health and safety, start/finish times in the preceding week and month, and fatigue breaks.
- **Roster publication**: **at least 14 days** before operation, **28 days where possible**, dated (cl 18.3(b)-(c)); may be altered for **circumstances the employer could not reasonably have foreseen, e.g. unplanned illness or emergency** (cl 18.3(d)); a resulting vacant shift is to be offered to suitable employees **on an equitable basis preferring permanent and direct employment** (cl 18.3(e)).
- **Shift limits** (cl 18.4): **no more than five shifts per week / ten per fortnight**; **not more than six consecutive days**; **four days per fortnight free, at least two consecutive**; **two consecutive days off (including on-call) in each 28 days not preceded by a night shift**; **every second weekend free where practicable**; spread of a shift **not more than 10 hours** unless a 12-hour agreement exists.
- **Break between shifts: 10 hours** (may be reduced by HSUWA agreement but **never below eight**) (cl 18.5(a)); **shift swaps** allowed with prior written approval provided no overtime/extra cost results (cl 18.5(b)).
- **Part-time**: variation of ordinary hours requires written consent and **48 hours' notice**, otherwise overtime (cl 13.1); **minimum three-hour shift** (cl 13.6); **Part-time flexibility for relief / relief pool**: part-timers who opt in are paid base rate plus penalties (no overtime) for extra hours **up to 76 hrs/fortnight without prior notice**; they may refuse any offered hours (cl 13.7). Requests to change hours answered in **21 days** (cl 13.8(e)).
- **Casual**: **25% loading**, **minimum three consecutive hours**, one hour's notice; default shift length 7.6 hrs for overtime purposes; no split shifts (cl 14).
- Contracting/agency: direct employment preferred; employer works to minimise agency hire (cl 15).

### 4.2 Annual leave (cl 39)
- **Four consecutive weeks** per 12 months, **plus 3.5 days per year in lieu of 17.5% leave loading for non-shift workers** (cl 39.1). Shift workers instead receive a **loading equal to the higher of the penalties they would have received or 20% of base**, convertible to four extra days (cl 39.13).
- May be taken in **hours, days or weeks** by agreement; **no employee can be required to take less than four consecutive weeks**; employees expected to take leave in the year following accrual; scheduling by consultation; **employer responds within 14 days**, refusal in writing with reasons; employer may **roster the employee off** if they refuse to discuss leave; **leave accrued more than 12 months earlier can be taken at any time suitable to the employee on two weeks' notice** (cl 39.2).
- **Excess leave (>2 years' entitlement)**: employee may be required to take sufficient leave before the next entitlement falls due (cl 39.4); failing that, excess may be paid out provided at least two weeks' leave is taken each year (cl 39.5); may be required to take double the entitlement in a year (cl 39.7).
- **Additional leave**: Sunday/public holiday shift workers 1-5 days by shift count (7-13 shifts = 1 day ... 35+ = 5 days) transitioning to a **Continuous Shift Worker accrual of 2.92 hours per fortnight** where at least four qualifying shifts are worked (max 38 hrs/yr); **on-call** 7.6 hrs per 120 rostered on-call hours up to 38 hrs; combined cap **38 hours** (cl 39.11). North of 26th +1 week (cl 39.12).
- **Purchased leave 42/52 to 51/52** (cl 39.19); **double leave at half pay or half at double pay** (cl 39.20); **cash out one or two weeks** if four weeks remain (cl 39.21).
- **Christmas/New Year closedown**: **three months' minimum notice**, up to **five days' annual leave** may be rostered, detail given **one month prior** (cl 42).
- Public holidays: 11 days incl. Easter Sunday (cl 41.1); shift workers on Christmas Day falling on a weekend paid 2.5x (cl 41.2).

### 4.3 Personal leave (cl 43)
- **114 hours per year** (64.6 on appointment, 49.4 at six months, then 114 per anniversary), cumulative, hourly (cl 43.8-43.11); sick/carer's leave paid including shift/weekend penalties (cl 43.2); anticipate up to 38 hours (cl 43.19); planned personal leave by prior approval, not for regular ongoing situations (cl 43.22(d)); reasonable notice, else as early as possible on the day (cl 43.25). Evidence rules follow the standard two-consecutive-day threshold (cl 43.26-43.30 – **verify exact wording**).

### 4.4 Long service leave (cl 47)
- **13 weeks after 10 years, then each further seven years** (cl 47.1); minimum one day; half/double pay (cl 47.2); cleared at employer convenience **within three years** of falling due, accumulation approved up to **26 weeks**; employer may roster the employee off if they refuse to discuss (cl 47.3); **pro-rata after seven years at 6.5 days per year** (cl 47.5).

### 4.5 Professional development leave (cl 54)
- **Health Professionals (cl 21.5 classifications): 32 hours per year** non-cumulative, not convertible to payment, pro-rata for part-time; travel time excluded (cl 54.4). Other registered/studying employees: **16 hours per year** (cl 54.5). Study leave also available (cl 53).

### 4.6 Overtime, on call, penalties, higher duties (cl 19, 20, 25)
- **Overtime**: **time and a half for the first three hours, double time thereafter**; **double time** between midnight-0600, Saturday after noon, and Sundays; **double time and a half** on public holidays (cl 19.1-19.2); overtime under 30 minutes on a day not paid (cl 19.3); TOIL on request, up to five days per year if requested in writing (cl 19.4); employees above Level G-8/P-3 get TOIL only unless rostered for regular overtime/on-call (cl 19.5).
- **On call**: **18.75% of 1/38th of the P-1.1 weekly base rate** per hour (cl 19.9(e)); on-call roster published **at least 14 days** ahead (cl 19.9(c)); **no on-call on the day before annual or long service leave** unless agreed (cl 19.9(j)); **recall minimum three hours** at overtime rates (cl 19.10); remote recall minimum one hour (cl 19.11); **10-hour break** after on-call overtime before next ordinary duty or double rate until released (cl 19.12).
- **Shift penalties**: **afternoon 15%**; **night 25%, rising to 30% from 1 July 2025 and 35% from 1 July 2026**; **Saturday 50%**; **Sunday 75%** (to 0700 Monday); weekend rates substitute for afternoon/night (cl 20.2-20.3). Casuals get base + casual loading + shift loading (cl 20.5).
- **Higher duties allowance** after **five consecutive working days** or more in a higher position (single days by agreement where day-by-day relief is a regular feature); full salary difference or proportion (cl 25.1-25.4); allowance continues on leave after 12 months' acting (cl 25.6).
- **Workload management** (cl 65): staffing appropriate for safe care; **leave cover arrangements will be considered** when reviewing workload issues (cl 65.5).

## 5. Medical Practitioners – AMA Industrial Agreement 2024

### 5.1 Doctors in Training (Part 2, cl 9-19)
- **Contracts** normally **12 months** (interns three years; training-program length for accredited trainees) (cl 9(1)); probation six months (cl 9(2)); **clinical rotations (including location) advised at least four weeks before each year begins**, preferences accommodated subject to operations, changes only after consultation (cl 9(9)); secondments by agreement with supervision (cl 9(8)).
- **Part-time**: minimum hours in writing, worked in **minimum continuous periods of three hours**; additional hours by agreement (cl 10(4)). **Casual**: **minimum three hours**, **25% loading**, three hours' notice; interns never casual, RMOs not normally (cl 11).
- **Hours of duty (cl 15)**: full-time **average 40 hours/week**; minimum time off: **eight days free from ordinary hours per 28 days, where practicable four free of all duty including on-call**; **at least two consecutive days off all duty (incl. on-call) per 28 days not preceded by a night shift**; **48 consecutive hours free after not more than 12 days' work**; **12 evenings off (Mon-Fri 1800-0800) per 28 days**; **every second weekend free where practicable** (cl 15(3)). **Rosters must provide at least a 10-hour break** between rostered duty; resuming before 10 hours off attracts a **50% loading until released for 10 hours** (cl 15(4)). Rostered hours **over 10 in a shift** are paid at extended-shift rates (cl 15(5)). **Minimum three-hour rostered periods; not more than 75 hours in any seven days and not more than 140 hours in any 14 days; not more than 14 consecutive hours; 12 consecutive hours if starting after noon (13 by AMA agreement); not normally more than four consecutive nights (five if total rostered hours are 50 or less); 24 hours free after 1-3 nights, 48 hours after 4-5 nights** (cl 15(6)). Paid 30-minute rest break per period of duty, a second if >10 hours (cl 15(7)). Taxi home if too tired to drive (cl 15(10)).
- **Rosters (cl 16)**: 14- or 28-day cycles aligned to pay periods; **no split shifts**; **not rostered on the night immediately before approved leave** except in exceptional circumstances (cl 16(4)); roster covers **not less than 28 days**, provided **at least 14 days** prior, **21 days where possible** (cl 16(5)); **not amended during currency except in emergency or with the practitioner's agreement**; practitioners may **swap rostered duty among themselves** with Head of Department approval not unreasonably refused (cl 16(6)); "emergency" includes an unanticipated change in demand or **an atypical level of unplanned absences**, and services must plan for a typical level of absence (cl 16(7)).
- **Extended shifts and overtime (cl 17)**: paid hours **over 10 in a shift**: Mon-Sat **150% for first three hours then 200%**, Sunday **200%**; part-time shift extensions at 150%/200%; paid hours **over 80 per fortnight at 150%, over 120 at 200%**; TOIL by written agreement; un-rostered overtime authorisation not unreasonably refused.
- **Professional development leave (cl 18)**: **three weeks' paid PDL per calendar year** (inclusive of **four days' exam leave** and **three clear days free of duty before an exam**); accrues weekly; **carry-over to a maximum of nine weeks**; applications **at least two months before** leave; employer replies in writing **within four weeks**; single days debited at eight hours or rostered hours; special leave up to 12 months at discretion.
- **Higher duties**: paid after **more than ten consecutive working days** acting (cl 19).

### 5.2 Senior Practitioners (Part 3, cl 20-31)
- Permanent employment preferred; **fixed term only** for relief (e.g. parental leave cover), finite projects/external funding, temporary vacancy during recruitment, fixed-duration visa, limited registration, or as agreed; Directors on **five-year** fixed terms (cl 20(3)); casual only for irregular hours or up to six months (cl 20(4)).
- **Hours (cl 22)**: no fixed hours; **average 40 hours/week professional commitment**, **80% clinical service and teaching**; on-call/recall not part of the ordinary commitment.
- **PDL (cl 30)**: **three weeks per year**, accumulates **not more than two years**; **overseas PDL: five additional weeks after each five years**, max 10 weeks in one period; written report required; **not more than 50% of the medical staff of any specialty/department absent on PDL at one time** (cl 30(4)); PD allowance (Arrangement A) $31,190 rising to $33,331; Arrangement B expenses allowance 3% of Consultant Y6 base.
- **Staffing (cl 31)**: levels set by the employer against benchmarks; vacancies to be filled as soon as practicable in consultation with the Head of Department.

### 5.3 General provisions (Part 4)
- **Penalties (cl 32)**: weekday **1800-2400: 20%**; weekday **0000-0800: 25%**; **Saturday 50%**; **midnight Saturday to 0800 Monday: 75%**; **public holiday 150%**, or 50% plus a day in lieu by agreement (max five banked).
- **On call and recall (cl 33)**: rostered by the Head of Department by clinical need; **no practitioner required to be on call more than one day in three** without AMA agreement or emergency (cl 33(1)(b)); DIT on-call **$12.80/hr** (18.75% of Registrar Y4), senior **$24.35/hr** (2024 rates, indexed); once rostered, the **onus is on the practitioner to negotiate swaps with peers or cover the roster themselves**, not the manager, at no extra cost (except sickness/personal emergency) (cl 33(1)(e)); on-call rosters posted **14 days** ahead where practicable (cl 33(1)(f)); DIT recall **minimum three hours** at 150% (day), 175% (Sunday), 200% (midnight-0600), 200% beyond three hours (cl 33(2)(b)).
- **Annual leave (cl 34)**: **160 hours (four weeks)** per year, accrues weekly; debited at rostered hours (cl 34(1)-(2)); **additional leave**: **eight hours per completed 120 hours rostered on call** (max 40 hrs) and **eight hours per seven ordinary Sunday/public-holiday shifts** (max 40 hrs), **combined maximum 40 hours** (cl 34(3)-(4)); portions of **one day or less** by agreement (cl 34(6)); in advance up to four weeks (cl 34(8)); HoD/Medical Director reviews balances annually (cl 34(10)); **employer confirms in writing within two weeks** of a written application (cl 34(11)); deferral approvals last one year; over three years' accrual lets the employer impose conditions (cl 34(12)); **more than two years' accrual: written notice to reduce within 12 months under an agreed leave management plan** (cl 34(13)); employer ensures adequate staffing, but **leave is not back-filled for periods of two weeks or less except on urgent clinical or service grounds with delegated approval** (cl 34(14)); excess over one year may be cashed out (cl 34(15)); **leave accrued more than 12 months earlier cannot be refused if two weeks' written notice is given** (cl 34(16)); **leave loading is annualised into base salary** (cl 34(17)); purchased leave 42/52 (cl 34(23)); half pay (cl 34(24)); cash out one or two weeks (cl 34(25)).
- **Public holidays (cl 35)**: 11 days; PH during annual leave observed on the next working day after the leave (cl 35(3)); on-call on a PH earns a day in lieu (cl 35(6)).
- **Personal leave (cl 36)**: **80 hours per year**, accrues weekly, cumulative (cl 36(4),(11)); paid including rostered extended-shift/overtime amounts but excluding penalties (cl 36(6)); hourly (cl 36(8)); first-year advance access (cl 36(10)); **medical certificate/evidence for more than two consecutive working days** (cl 36(14)).
- **Long service leave (cl 38)**: **13 weeks after 10 years, then after each seven years**; pro-rata access after seven years by agreement; **at least three months' notice** of intention to take LSL (cl 38(1)(d)); taken at employer convenience **within three years** of falling due (cl 38(3)-(4)); half/double pay in weekly multiples (cl 38(5)); fixed-term practitioners with five+ years get pro-rata on non-renewal (cl 38(1)(e)).

## 8. How the cover-recommendation agent should use these rules

1. **Classify the requester and each candidate by agreement** using the mapping table; every constraint below is keyed to the agreement.
2. **Leave approval workflow constraints**: the agent must produce a decision that the manager can deliver inside the mandatory response window (14 days ANF/UWU/HSUWA; 2 weeks AMA) and, if declining, must generate written reasons citing specific operational requirements (ANF cl 33(1)(d)-(e); HSUWA 39.2(e)). "Operational requirements" for purchased leave/flexible work are contractually limited to leave-cover availability, service impact and leave liability, so the explanation should be framed in those terms.
3. **Cannot-decline situations**: leave accrued more than 12 months ago with two weeks' notice (HSUWA 39.2(g); AMA 34(16)); employer-side pressure to clear excess balances (ANF >10 weeks; HSUWA/UWU/AMA >2 years). The agent should score requests from over-accrued staff higher and flag the employer's own obligation to clear them.
4. **Cover candidate filtering (hard constraints)**: rest breaks (9.5/10/20 hours), maximum consecutive shifts/days, maximum shifts per fortnight, maximum shift length, no split shifts, weekend-off frequency, DIT 75/140-hour caps and night-run limits, no night before approved leave (AMA), no on-call the day before leave (ANF/HSUWA), casual minimum engagement lengths, part-time notice requirements (HSUWA 48 hours; UWU one day), roster-change rules (AMA rosters cannot be amended once current except emergency/agreement), and higher-duties eligibility for acting-up cover.
5. **Cost model (cost factors)**: rank options roughly as: (a) redeploy permanent relief-pool/FTE staff at ordinary rate; (b) offer additional hours to opted-in part-timers (HSUWA 13.7 base + penalties; ANF ordinary rate + accrual or casual loading); (c) casual pool at base + 25% + penalties (min 2-3 hours); (d) shift swaps by peers at no cost (HSUWA 18.5(b), AMA 16(6)); (e) overtime at 1.5x/2x/2.5x with recall minimums and mandatory post-overtime rest that may knock the person out of their next shift; (f) agency only as last resort (ANF 16(2)). Apply the correct shift penalty to each candidate shift (afternoon/night/Saturday/Sunday/public holiday), noting HSUWA night loading steps to 35% from 1 July 2026 and AMA uses 20%/25% evening/night bands.
6. **Safe staffing gate**: for ANF-covered wards in the Ratio Model, the recommended roster must still meet 1:4 (+coordinator) mornings/afternoons and 1:7 nights on medical/surgical wards (policy MP 0187/24 – figures not verified from the policy text), or the NHpPD category level elsewhere; ratios count RN/M/EN only, so AIN cover cannot substitute for a ratio position. For AMA senior departments, no more than 50% of a specialty may be on PDL at once.
7. **Explanations when dates cannot be accommodated** should cite: the specific staffing minimum breached, which candidates were excluded by which rule (with clause), the cost/feasibility of the remaining options, and alternative dates or partial approvals (e.g. split into a two-week block as ANF 33(16) contemplates, or half-pay/double-period options).
